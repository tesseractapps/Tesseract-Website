# Your Workforce, Under Control.

Manage the full support worker lifecycle including onboarding, credentials, leave, training compliance, and expiry alerts.

## The Problem

A support worker with an [expired first aid certificate is a compliance risk](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness). A support worker you don’t know has an expired first aid certificate is a crisis waiting to happen. With workers across multiple sites, manual tracking guarantees something slips. 

## What Matters Most

- Every credential tracked with automated expiry alerts at 90 days, 30 days, and expiry
- Onboarding workflows that get support workers job-ready faster
- Leave balances and availability visible to roster coordinators in real time
- Training compliance monitored across the workforce
- One profile per worker with qualifications, training, incidents, and performance

## How We Solve This

TesseractApps manages every stage of the support worker lifecycle from a single profile. Onboarding workflows guide new workers through document submission, credential verification, and [mandatory training](/capabilities/learning-management). Automated alerts notify managers 90 days before any credential expires and prevent [rostering of workers with lapsed credentials](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling). [Leave management](/capabilities/staff-self-service-portal) integrates directly with rostering, so approved leave automatically blocks availability. 

## What You Get

- Centralised worker profiles - qualifications, credentials, training history, incident records 
- Automated credential expiry alerts at 90 day, 30 day, and expiry milestones
- Configurable onboarding workflows with document upload and verification steps 
- Leave management integrated with rostering and availability
- Training compliance tracking with completion and renewal reminders
- Qualification matrix showing coverage gaps across your workforce 
- Bulk credential reporting for audit preparation 

## Is This Right for You?

- You track credentials in spreadsheets or shared drives
- A lapsed credential has slipped through to a participant-facing shift
- Onboarding a new support worker takes more than a week of admin effort
- You cannot produce a credential compliance report in under five minutes
