# The Right Data. The Right Person. Right Now.

Role-specific dashboards for support workers, managers, compliance, finance, and executives with real-time operational visibility.

## The Problem

You either have too much data and no clarity, or not enough data and no confidence. [Reports are generated manually](/capabilities/accounting-financial-reporting), distributed by email, and outdated by the time someone reads them. 

## What Matters Most

- Role-specific dashboards - every person sees what they need 
- Real-time data refresh - no lag between activity and visibility 
- Executive reporting without manual compilation
- Drill-down from organisation to site to individual
- One source of truth across workforce compliance and finance

## How We Solve This

TesseractApps delivers role-specific dashboards that surface the right data to the right person. A [support worker](/solutions/support-worker) sees their schedule. A [compliance lead](/solutions/compliance-lead) sees risk indicators. A [finance manager](/solutions/finance-manager) sees margin data. An executive sees the entire organisation. All dashboards draw from the same real-time data layer. 

## What You Get

- Support worker dashboard for shifts, credential status, and reminders
- Roster manager dashboard for coverage, overtime, and expiries
- Compliance Lead: credential expiry dashboard, open incidents, audit readiness, CAPA status 
- Claiming pipeline, budget utilisation, revenue tracking, reconciliation exceptions 
- Margin by service line, occupancy rates, compliance posture, workforce KPIs 

## Is This Right for You?

- Different roles do not have role-specific visibility
- Executive reporting is manually compiled from multiple sources
- Compliance monitoring still relies on spreadsheets
- Finance lacks real-time labour cost and margin visibility
