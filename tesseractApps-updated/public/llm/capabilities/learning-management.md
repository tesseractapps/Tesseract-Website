# Training that lives inside your operations.

TesseractApps manages your workforce. WYZED manages your training. Together, every learning record, completion status, and compliance requirement sits inside the same platform your team already uses � connected to the roster, the role, and the worker profile.

## The Problem

A new support worker joins. Their profile is set up in TesseractApps. Their induction checklist lives in an email thread. Their [mandatory NDIS modules](/capabilities/workforce-management) are tracked in a spreadsheet, or not tracked at all. By the time they start their first shift, nobody knows with certainty what they have or have not completed. That is a compliance risk. When the [NDIS Commission](/ndis-glossary) asks for evidence of training, the answer is scattered across three systems and someone's inbox.

## What Matters Most

- Every worker's training status is visible without switching platforms
- Induction is triggered automatically when a new worker is created
- Mandatory NDIS modules are mapped to the worker's role, not assigned manually
- Certification expiry is tracked and renewals are flagged before they lapse
- Training records are audit-ready without manual compilation

## How We Solve This

WYZED is integrated directly into TesseractApps. When a [new worker is created](/capabilities/workforce-management), their role triggers a WYZED induction pathway automatically � modules assigned, deadlines set, no manual setup required. Workers complete training without leaving TesseractApps. Completion status feeds back into the [roster](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling) so managers know who is cleared before a shift is published. [Certificate expiry](/capabilities/workforce-management) is tracked and renewal pathways are triggered automatically. Every completion, assessment result, and timestamp is stored and connected to the worker profile.

## What You Get

- 86+ NDIS-mapped modules from the WYZED library
- Role-based learning pathways assigned automatically on hire
- Induction triggered when a new worker profile is created
- Training completion visible inside TesseractApps rostering
- Certificate and qualification expiry tracking with automatic renewal triggers
- Upload your own BSPs, policies, and procedures alongside WYZED modules
- Knowledge checks and assessment results stored against the worker profile
- Audit-ready training records connected to the worker profile

## Is This Right for You?

- Training compliance is tracked in spreadsheets or email threads
- You cannot see a worker's training status without leaving TesseractApps
- Induction is set up manually each time a new worker joins
- Certifications lapse because expiry dates are not tracked centrally
- Preparing training evidence for an NDIS audit requires manual compilation
