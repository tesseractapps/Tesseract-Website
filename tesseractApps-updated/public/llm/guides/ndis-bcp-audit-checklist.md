# Business Continuity Plan (BCP) Audit Checklist

Most providers have a BCP document. Few can prove it works. Download our free BCP Audit Checklist to test your plan against what NDIS auditors now expect in 2026, including risk scenarios, participant communication, staff training, and plan governance.

**Topic:** SIL

**Best for:** SIL Providers and Compliance Managers

## Having a BCP Is Not Enough

The 2026 NDIS Practice Standards have shifted from checking whether a BCP document exists to verifying that the BCP system actually works. Auditors now expect evidence of testing, training, participant communication protocols, and regular reviews.

The most common audit findings:

- Generic risk scenarios (e.g. 'natural disaster' with no specifics)
- No documented participant communication protocol
- Training policy exists but no completion records
- Plan has never been tested or updated since first written
- No named person responsible for maintaining the plan

## What You Get

Your free BCP audit checklist covers 6 sections:

- Risk Scenarios: Are your risks specific to your service, with likelihood and impact assessed?
- Response Protocols: Does each risk have specific actions, named owners, and escalation pathways?
- Participant Communication: Do you have a documented protocol for notifying participants during disruption?
- Recovery and Review: Is your post-incident process defined, with lessons learned captured?
- Staff Training: Can you produce training completion records for every staff member?
- Plan Governance: Is there a named owner, annual review schedule, and tabletop exercise evidence?
- Fillable PDF format: type directly into each section and add your own notes
