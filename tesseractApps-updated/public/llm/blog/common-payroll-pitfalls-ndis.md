# Avoid These Common Payroll Pitfalls: A Guide for NDIS Providers

Payroll management is one of the most complex and consequential aspects of running an NDIS provider business.

Payroll management is one of the most complex and consequential aspects of running an NDIS provider business. Mistakes can lead to underpayment claims, ATO penalties, staff dissatisfaction, and compliance failures. Yet many providers continue to struggle with the same recurring issues.

Here are the seven most common payroll pitfalls — and how TesseractApps helps you avoid them:

## 1. Incorrect Employee Classification

Misclassifying employees as casual when they are entitled to permanent status, or incorrectly applying award levels, can result in significant underpayment liability. The SCHADS Award is complex, and getting classification right is critical.

TesseractApps automates award interpretation, ensuring employees are classified correctly and paid at the right rate from day one.

## 2. Late or Inaccurate Payments

Late payment of wages is a breach of the Fair Work Act and can result in penalties. Inaccurate payments — whether through timesheet errors, incorrect allowances, or missed overtime — erode staff trust and create legal risk.

Our integrated timesheets and automated payroll processing ensure payments are accurate and on time, every time.

## 3. Failure to Stay Updated with Payroll Regulations

Payroll regulations change regularly — [minimum wage increases](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment), superannuation rate changes, STP Phase 2 requirements, and SCHADS Award updates all require prompt action.

TesseractApps updates its payroll engine automatically when regulations change, keeping your organisation compliant without manual intervention.

## 4. Poor Record-Keeping

Inadequate [payroll records](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness) are a common audit finding. Providers must retain pay slips, timesheets, and employment records for at least seven years under the Fair Work Act.

TesseractApps stores all payroll records securely in the cloud, making them instantly accessible for audits or employee queries.

## 5. Overlooking Employee Entitlements

Missed leave accruals, unpaid allowances, and incorrect [penalty rates](/ndis-glossary) are common sources of underpayment. These errors, even when unintentional, can result in costly back-pay obligations.

Our platform automatically calculates all entitlements based on employment type, award classification, and hours worked — eliminating the risk of oversight.

## 6. Lack of System Integration

When [rostering](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling), [timesheets](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment), and payroll operate in separate systems, data must be manually transferred between them — creating opportunities for errors and inconsistencies.

TesseractApps integrates rostering, attendance, and payroll in a single platform, eliminating manual data transfer and ensuring consistency across all records.

## 7. Neglecting Data Security

Payroll data is highly sensitive. Inadequate security controls can expose employee financial information to unauthorised access — with serious privacy and legal consequences.

TesseractApps is built on [Salesforce](/capabilities/salesforce-native-architecture) with enterprise-grade security, AES-256 encryption, and role-based access controls — protecting your payroll data at every level.
