# Signatures Without the Paper Chase

Collect legally binding digital signatures on service agreements, consent forms, and compliance documents without paper workflows.

## The Problem

[Service agreements](/capabilities/participant-management) sit in email inboxes waiting to be printed, signed, scanned, and filed. Consent forms go missing. Workers sign documents with no version control. You spend hours chasing signatures that should take seconds. 

## What Matters Most

- Service agreements and consent forms signed digitally
- Signature requests sent and tracked within the platform
- Signed documents automatically filed against participant or worker records
- Version control ensuring correct document versions are signed
- Legal validity equivalent to wet signatures

## How We Solve This

T-Sign enables digital signature collection on any document within TesseractApps. Service agreements, consent forms, incident acknowledgements, and policy documents are sent for signature directly from [participant or worker profiles](/capabilities/participant-management). Signed documents are automatically filed, timestamped, and linked to the relevant record. 

## What You Get

- Digital signature collection on service agreements, consent forms, and policies
- Signature requests sent directly from participant and worker profiles 
- Automatic filing to relevant records on completion
- Signature tracking dashboard showing pending, completed, and overdue documents 
- Multi-party signing for documents requiring more than one signature 
- Legally compliant signatures with full audit trail
- Template library for frequently signed documents

## Is This Right for You?

- Service agreements are printed, signed on paper, and scanned back into the system 
- You chase signatures on consent forms for days or weeks 
- Signed documents are not consistently filed against participant records 
- You need a faster, auditable process for document execution
