# Your Books. Always in Sync.

Bi-directional Xero integration syncing invoices, payments, and finance data without manual CSV workflows.

## The Problem

Your operational platform and [accounting system do not talk to each other](/capabilities/accounting-financial-reporting). Your finance team exports data from one system and imports it into Xero manually. Double-entry creates errors. Reconciliation delays mean you are always looking at last month’s financial position. 

## What Matters Most

- Invoices and payments synced to Xero automatically
- No manual CSV exports or data re-entry 
- Chart of accounts mapped to NDIS service categories
- Real-time financial position instead of month-end reconciliation 
- Error reduction through elimination of manual data transfer 

## How We Solve This

TesseractApps connects directly to Xero. Invoices generated from [NDIS claiming](/capabilities/ndis-claiming-invoicing) flow to your general ledger automatically. Payments received are matched. Your chart of accounts is mapped to operational categories, so [financial reporting reflects your service structure](/capabilities/accounting-financial-reporting). 

## What You Get

- Bi-directional sync between TesseractApps and Xero
- Automatic invoice posting to general ledger
- Payment matching and reconciliation
- Chart of accounts mapping to NDIS service categories
- Real-time dashboards reflecting synced financial data
- Error logging and sync status monitoring
- Configurable sync frequency and approval rules

## Is This Right for You?

- Your finance team manually transfers data between your operational platform and Xero 
- Invoice reconciliation takes more than a day each month
- Manual imports create data-entry errors
- You want real-time financial visibility without waiting for month-end processing
