# TesseractApps at the Melbourne Expo 2025 Driving Smarter NDIS Operations

TesseractApps showcased its platform at the Melbourne Expo 2025, demonstrating how NDIS providers can streamline operations and deliver outstanding care.

TesseractApps was proud to showcase its platform at the Melbourne Expo 2025, connecting with NDIS providers from across Victoria and beyond. The expo was a fantastic opportunity to demonstrate how our integrated software can help providers streamline operations, stay compliant, and deliver outstanding care.

## Streamline Your NDIS Operations

### Participant Management

Our comprehensive [participant management](/capabilities/participant-management) module gives coordinators a complete view of each participant's care journey — goals, budgets, service history, and progress notes in one secure platform.

### Rostering & Payroll

Automate your [rostering](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling) with smart scheduling that considers staff availability, qualifications, and participant preferences. Integrated payroll processing eliminates double-handling and award interpretation errors.

### Cost & Service Tracking

Real-time [financial dashboards](/capabilities/dashboards-reporting) provide visibility into budget utilisation, funding gaps, and [NDIA claim](/capabilities/ndis-claiming-invoicing) status — enabling proactive financial management.

### Mobile Access

The TesseractApps mobile app gives support workers everything they need on the go — their schedule, care plans, [GPS](/capabilities/clock-in-out) clock-in/out, and communication tools.

## ISO Certified Security

TesseractApps is certified to both [ISO 27001](/capabilities/salesforce-native-architecture) (information security) and ISO 9001 (quality management) standards, providing providers with confidence that their data and operations are protected at the highest level.
