# Never Miss an Incident Reporting Deadline Again.

Mobile incident logging, automatic severity classification, escalation workflows, CAPA management, and NDIS Commission reporting support. 

## The Problem

A support worker witnesses an incident at 7pm. They write it on a piece of paper. It reaches the coordinator two days later. By then, the [24-hour NDIS Commission reporting window](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness) has closed - and you are explaining why.

## What Matters Most

- Incidents captured at the point they occur - on a mobile device, with guided prompts
- Severity classification applied automatically based on incident type and impact
- Reportable incidents escalated immediately to the right people, every time
- Investigations tracked from assignment through CAPA completion
- A complete incident register that satisfies the NDIS Commission without manual assembly

## How We Solve This

Support workers log incidents on their phone the moment they occur. The system classifies each incident by severity, determines whether it is reportable, and triggers the appropriate [escalation workflow](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness). Managers receive instant notifications for reportable incidents. Investigation workflows guide teams through root cause analysis, witness statements, and [corrective actions](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness).

## What You Get

- Mobile incident logging with guided fields and automatic severity classification
- Escalation engine with configurable notification rules by incident type
- Investigation workflows with task assignment, evidence collection, timeline tracking
- CAPA management - corrective and preventive actions tracked to completion
- Incident analytics - trends, repeat incidents, and open investigation dashboards
- NDIS Commission reporting support with data formatted for submission requirements

## Is This Right for You?

- You have missed or nearly missed a 24-hour NDIS Commission reporting deadline
- Incident reports arrive days after the event
- Investigation outcomes and CAPA actions are tracked in emails or spreadsheets
- Your incident process relies on individual knowledge rather than system-guided workflows
