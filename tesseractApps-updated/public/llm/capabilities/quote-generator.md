# Quote Accurately. Every Time.

Generate NDIS service quotes aligned to price guides, funding categories, and participant plans in minutes.

## The Problem

Creating a service quote means cross-referencing the [NDIS price guide](/ndis-glossary), checking [funding categories](/capabilities/ndis-claiming-invoicing), calculating hours, and formatting a document. It takes longer than it should, and errors in quoting lead to billing disputes and revenue leakage. 

## What Matters Most

- Quotes generated from current NDIS price guide rates automatically 
- Funding category alignment built into quote creation
- Professional quote documents ready for participants and plan managers
- Quote-to-service-agreement conversion without re-entry
- Historical quote tracking for reporting and analysis

## How We Solve This

The Quote Generator pulls current [NDIS price guide rates](/capabilities/ndis-claiming-invoicing) and participant funding information to produce accurate, professional quotes. Select the support categories, enter the hours, and the system calculates the costs. Approved quotes convert directly to [service agreements](/capabilities/participant-management) - no rekeying required. 

## What You Get

- Automated quote generation from NDIS price guide rates
- Funding category and support item alignment
- Professional quote document templates
- Direct quote-to-service-agreement conversion
- Quote versioning and approval workflows
- Historical quote analytics and conversion tracking
- Bulk quoting for multi-participant service proposals 

## Is This Right for You?

- Quoting takes more than 15 minutes per participant
- You have sent quotes with incorrect NDIS price guide rates
- Approved quotes are manually re-entered to create agreements
- You lack visibility into quote conversion patterns
