# NDIS Registration Readiness Checklist

Download our free Registration Readiness Checklist and get a clear, step-by-step roadmap to becoming a registered NDIS provider. Covers governance, policies, workforce screening, incident management, participant supports, and quality systems.

### Why This Matters

**The deadline is real.**

From 1 July 2026, every SIL provider must be registered with the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission. There is no grace period. Unregistered providers will not be able to deliver NDIS-funded supports.

**Registration takes time.**

The typical registration process takes 8-12 weeks from application to certification. If you have not started, every week counts.

**The checklist keeps you on track.**

Our Registration Readiness Checklist breaks the process into manageable sections with specific actions to complete. Use it to track your progress and identify gaps before they become problems.
