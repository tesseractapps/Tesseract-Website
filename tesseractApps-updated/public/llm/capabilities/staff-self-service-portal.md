# Give Your Team the Tools to Manage Themselves

Empower support workers with shift visibility, document uploads, leave requests, and credential management - all from one portal.

## The Problem

Support workers call the office for everything - shift details, leave balances, payslip queries, [credential renewals](/capabilities/workforce-management). Every call pulls a coordinator away from higher-value work. Workers feel disconnected. Coordinators feel overwhelmed. 

## What Matters Most

- Support workers can view upcoming shifts, swap requests, and availability 
- ave requests submitted and approved without phone calls or emails 
- Credential and document uploads managed directly by the worker 
- Payslip and timesheet visibility without admin involvement
- Reduced routine administrative burden on office staff

## How We Solve This

The Staff Self-Service Portal gives every support worker a single place to manage their operational relationship with your organisation. They see their [roster](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling), submit leave, upload [credentials](/capabilities/workforce-management), view [timesheets](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment), and receive notifications - all from their phone. Coordinators handle exceptions, not routine enquiries. 

## What You Get

- Personal dashboard with upcoming shifts and schedule changes
- Leave request submission and approval tracking
- Credential and document upload with expiry notifications
- Timesheet review and approval visibility
- Shift swap requests with qualification validation
- Push notifications for roster changes, approvals, and reminders
- Availability management linked to rostering

## Is This Right for You?

- Support workers regularly contact the office for basic schedule information
- Leave requests are managed through email, text, or phone calls
- Credential renewals require admin staff to chase workers
- You want to reduce routine administrative workload on coordinators
