# TesseractApps Connects with Canberra's NDIS Community at the Expo

TesseractApps, a proudly Canberra-based company, participated in the Canberra NDIS Expo, sharing how our platform supports disability service excellence.

As a proudly Canberra-based company, TesseractApps was thrilled to participate in the Canberra NDIS Expo — an event that brought together the heart of our local disability community.

The expo provided a fantastic opportunity to engage face-to-face with NDIS providers, [support coordinators](/solutions/support-coordination), and participants from across the ACT and surrounding regions. We had rich conversations about the unique challenges facing providers in our region and how TesseractApps can help.

## Connecting with Our Local Community

Being headquartered in Canberra means we have a special connection to the providers and participants in our backyard. The Canberra NDIS Expo gave us the chance to deepen these relationships and listen to what matters most to the local NDIS community.

From [compliance concerns](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness) to [workforce challenges](/capabilities/workforce-management), the conversations at the expo helped us understand how TesseractApps can continue to evolve to meet the needs of ACT providers.

## Looking Ahead

Following the success of the Canberra Expo, we're planning a series of webinars and one-on-one consultations to continue supporting Canberra-based NDIS providers. If you missed us at the expo, reach out to book a personalised demo and discover how TesseractApps can transform your operations.
