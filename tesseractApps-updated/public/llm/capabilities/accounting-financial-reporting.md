# See Where Your Money Goes. And Where It Doesn’t.

Xero integration, margin tracking, revenue leakage identification, and financial reporting by service line - built for NDIS providers.

## The Problem

Your [operational data lives in one system and your financials in another](/capabilities/dashboards-reporting). By the time your accountant reconciles the two, you’re looking at last month’s problems. You know some service lines are more profitable than others - but you’re guessing which ones.

## What Matters Most

- Manual reconciliation between systems eliminated
- Real-time margin visibility by service stream, site, and participant 
- Xero integration removing double entry
- Native payroll and accounting available at Enterprise (no third-party dependency) 
- Board-level reporting from live operational data

## How We Solve This

TesseractApps connects budgets, invoices, [payroll](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment), and [NDIS claiming](/capabilities/ndis-claiming-invoicing) in a single system. For providers using [Xero](/capabilities/xero-integration), data synchronises automatically. Margin analytics show exactly where your revenue is going. At [Enterprise stage](/solutions/enterprise), native payroll and accounting remove external dependencies entirely. 

## What You Get

- Xero integration - operational transactions flow to your general ledger automatically
- Native payroll and accounting at Enterprise tier for full financial consolidation
- Margin tracking by service line, location, and participant group 
- Revenue leakage identification - flagging unbilled services, unclaimed shifts, under-recovered costs 
- Financial dashboards with real-time revenue, cost, and margin views 
- Service line profitability reporting for strategic decision-making 

## Is This Right for You?

- You manually transfer data between operational and accounting systems
- Financial reporting takes more than a day to compile
- You cannot see margins by service stream or site in real time
- You operate multiple entities and need consolidated reporting
