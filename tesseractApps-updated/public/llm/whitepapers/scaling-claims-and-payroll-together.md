# Scaling Claims and Payroll Together

As the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS)
expands, you face deeper workforce challenges. The
Australian Government estimates about 128 000 more
workers will be needed by June 2025.
Your workforce management must improve if you are to
meet demand, maintain compliance, and manage costs.
This whitepaper offers clear, actionable strategies you
can apply now.
