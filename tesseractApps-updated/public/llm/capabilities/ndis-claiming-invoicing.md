# Claim Every Dollar You Have Earned.

Funding category alignment, budget tracking, 3-layer reconciliation, and PACE-aligned claiming. No more revenue leakage.

## The Problem

Your finance team manually cross-references [rosters against timesheets against claims](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment). Some shifts never get claimed. Some claims get rejected because the line item doesn’t match the funding category. 

## What Matters Most

- Services delivered are services claimed with no unbilled shifts
- Claim rejections reduced through pre-submission validation against funding rules 
- Budget tracking by participant, plan period, and support category in real time 
- 3-Layer Reconciliation catching discrepancies before they become lost revenue 
- PACE-aligned claiming that keeps providers ahead of changes

## How We Solve This

TesseractApps connects NDIS claiming directly to [service delivery records](/capabilities/rostering-scheduling), [timesheets](/capabilities/timesheets-payroll-alignment), and [participant plans](/capabilities/participant-management). Claims are generated from verified shift data with correct funding category alignment. [Three reconciliation layers](/capabilities/accounting-financial-reporting) - operational, payroll, and financial - ensure every dollar is accounted for. 

## What You Get

- Automated claim generation from approved timesheets mapped to funding categories
- Pre-submission validation against price guides and participant budgets
- 3-Layer Reconciliation: roster-to-timesheet → timesheet-to-claim → claim-to-payment 
- Real-time budget tracking by participant, plan period, and support category 
- Claim rejection analytics identifying recurring issues and root causes 
- PACE-aligned claiming workflows and bulk claiming for high-volume providers 

## Is This Right for You?

- You reconcile claims against service records manually
- Your NDIS claim rejection rate is above 2%
- You have discovered shifts delivered but never invoiced
- Participant funding balances are tracked in spreadsheets
