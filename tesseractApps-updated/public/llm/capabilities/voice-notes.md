# Speak It. It Is Recorded.

Capture progress notes by voice on mobile, transcribe automatically, and link to participant records in real time.

## The Problem

Support workers finish a shift and need to write [progress notes](/capabilities/participant-management). They are in the car, between appointments, or finishing a long day. Typing detailed notes on a phone is slow and error-prone. Notes get deferred, abbreviated, or skipped entirely. 

## What Matters Most

- Progress notes captured by voice at point of service
- Automatic transcription linked to participant records
- Notes structured for compliance - connected to goals and funding categories 
- Reduced documentation burden on support workers
- Higher quality and more detailed notes than manual typing 

## How We Solve This

Voice Notes lets support workers record progress notes by speaking into their mobile device. Recordings are automatically transcribed and linked to the relevant [participant record](/capabilities/participant-management). Notes can be reviewed, edited, and approved by coordinators. The result is faster documentation, richer detail, and higher compliance with [progress note requirements](/capabilities/compliance-audit-readiness). 

## What You Get

- Voice-to-text progress note capture on mobile
- Automatic transcription with review and edit capability
- Notes linked to participant profiles, goals, and funding categories
- Coordinator review and approval workflow
- Searchable note archive for audit and plan review preparation 
- Offline recording with automatic sync when connectivity returns 

## Is This Right for You?

- Support workers regularly defer or abbreviate progress notes 
- Progress note quality is inconsistent across your workforce 
- Documentation compliance is a recurring audit finding 
- Workers spend excessive time typing notes on mobile devices
