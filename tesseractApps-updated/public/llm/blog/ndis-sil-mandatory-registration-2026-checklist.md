# Mandatory SIL Registration from 1 July 2026: Your Complete Checklist

Mandatory NDIS SIL registration starts 1 July 2026. Download our complete registration readiness checklist and prepare step by step.

On 1 July 2026, the rules change. Supported Independent Living (SIL) providers who have operated outside the NDIS registration system will no longer be able to do so. The NDIS Minister confirmed this in December 2025, and it represents one of the most significant structural shifts the disability sector has seen.

If you are currently unregistered or unsure of your status, this is your definitive guide to what NDIS SIL mandatory registration involves, what you need to have in place, and how to avoid last-minute scrambling.

## Why Mandatory Registration Is Happening

The push for mandatory SIL registration comes directly from the 2023 NDIS Review and subsequent stakeholder consultations. Participants and families reported significant variation in the quality of SIL supports, and a lack of oversight for unregistered providers was identified as a key risk. Mandatory registration brings all SIL providers under the Commission's regulatory framework for the first time.

Platform providers, organisations that connect participants with support workers through digital platforms, are also required to register under the same deadline.

## What NDIS SIL Registration Requires

To become a registered NDIS provider, you must meet the requirements of the NDIS Practice Standards relevant to your registration group. For SIL providers, this includes a new dedicated SIL standard currently being finalised. At a high level, you will need to demonstrate:

- A governance structure with clear accountability
- Documented policies and procedures aligned to the Practice Standards
- A risk management framework with active monitoring
- Worker screening and credential verification processes
- An incident management system with reporting capability
- A business continuity plan
- Participant feedback mechanisms and continuous improvement processes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: When does mandatory NDIS SIL registration start?

A: 1 July 2026. All SIL providers must be registered by this date with no grace period.

### Q: How long does NDIS provider registration take?

A: Registration typically takes 8–12 weeks from application to certification. Starting early is essential.

### Q: What happens if I am not registered by July 2026?

A: Unregistered SIL providers will not be able to deliver NDIS-funded supports after 1 July 2026.

### Q: Are platform providers also required to register?

A: Yes. Platform providers that connect participants with support workers must also register by 1 July 2026.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Assuming the process is quick. Registration typically takes 8–12 weeks from application to certification.
- Treating documentation as a one-time exercise. Auditors want to see evidence of ongoing monitoring, not just a document written the day before the audit.
- Underestimating workforce requirements. Every worker needs current screening and training records.
- Using paper-based or spreadsheet systems that cannot produce audit-ready evidence quickly.
