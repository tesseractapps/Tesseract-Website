# Platform Provider Registration Readiness Guide

If your platform connects NDIS participants with support workers, mandatory registration starts 1 July 2026. Download our free Platform Provider Registration Guide for a plain-English walkthrough of what is required and how to prepare.

**Topic:** SIL

**Best for:** NDIS Platform Providers and Digital Marketplace Operators

## Are You a Platform Provider?

Answer these 3 questions:

- Does your business operate a website or app where NDIS participants find and book support workers?
- Do you take a fee, commission, or margin from transactions between participants and workers?
- Do the workers on your platform include independent contractors (not just employees)?

If you answered yes to any of these, this guide is for you.

## What You Get

Your free registration guide includes:

- A clear definition of who counts as a platform provider under the NDIS Commission
- 7-step registration readiness walkthrough
- Governance requirements specific to platform models
- Worker screening verification guide for contractor workforces
- Incident management requirements for platform-connected workers
- Participant protection and complaints mechanism checklist
- Evidence and documentation systems guide
- Fillable PDF: add your platform details, track progress, and note action items

> [!INFO]
> For most platform providers, worker screening is the most complex registration requirement. Unlike traditional providers who employ workers directly, platforms often work with independent contractors. This guide includes a practical framework for verifying and maintaining current screening across a large, distributed workforce.
